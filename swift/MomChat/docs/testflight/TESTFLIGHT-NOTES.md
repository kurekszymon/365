## Beta App Description (EN)

*App Store Connect → TestFlight → Test Information → Beta App Description*

> MomChat is a calm, private companion for pregnancy and the first three years with your baby. Ask anything, at any hour, and get a short, warm answer that knows your due date or your baby's age. Everything runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence: no account, no cloud, and your chats never leave your phone.
>
> MomChat gives information, not diagnosis. If something feels wrong, call your midwife, doctor or emergency number.
>
> Requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on, and the iPhone set to English.

## What to Test (EN), build 1

*App Store Connect → TestFlight → the build → Test Details → What to Test (max 4,000 characters)*

> Thank you for testing MomChat! 💜
>
> **Before you start:** turn on Apple Intelligence (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri) and set your iPhone to English. Answers are in English for now.
>
> **Please try:**
> 1. Set up your profile: expecting, baby is here, or both.
> 2. Ask the questions you'd really ask, the ones you'd type at 3am. Are the answers right for your week or your baby's age? Too long, too short, too cold?
> 3. Tap a starter suggestion on an empty chat.
> 4. If the app offers to save something to your profile, does it get it right?
> 5. Open your past chats, search them, and share one as a PDF.
> 6. Optional: turn on the Face ID lock in Settings, and try a backup and restore.
>
> **Free questions and purchases:** you get 20 free questions, then the plans screen. Buying in TestFlight is free (sandbox), nothing is charged, and subscriptions renew faster than in real life.
>
> **Safety:** if you type something urgent (bleeding, a baby not breathing, thoughts of harming yourself), MomChat shows emergency help instead of an answer. If it shows that screen when it shouldn't, or misses a case where it should, please tell us. That's the most valuable feedback of all. **This is a test app: in a real emergency, always call your emergency number.**
>
> **How to send feedback:** take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or use the TestFlight app. Please don't include health details you'd rather keep private: we only see what you send.
