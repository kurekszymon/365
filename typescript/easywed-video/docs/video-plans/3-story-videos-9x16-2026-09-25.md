# Three moments from a couple's wedding prep, 9:16

## Strategy

Polish engaged couples with a date, a venue and a guest list, reached on Reels / TikTok, where
they already get wedding content between everything else they are organising. The job is the
same as every earlier plan: open easywed.app and start a seating plan tonight. These three do
it from a different angle. The earlier cuts each demo one feature. These tell a moment the couple
has already had, or knows is coming (mum asking again, the parents who can't sit near each other,
the Sunday evening they finally sit down to it), and easywed is simply how it gets sorted. The
aim is a video someone forwards to their partner or their mum with *"to my"* ("that's us"). The
features on screen are the ones already proven. What's new is the situation around them, and two
beats that no film has used yet: the viewer invite link, and a whole table moved with its guests.

`keep-apart` ships first. It is the cheapest, it works entirely signed out, and a moved table with
its guests has never been shown. `mama-link` is the strongest share, but it is the only one that
needs an account, so it should follow a guest-mode video rather than lead the series.

**Other formats are out of scope this run** (the user asked for 9:16 only). None of these three
has a 16:9 loop or a 1:1 cut planned. A 1:1 would still cost the third `useFormat()` branch
and a third hall plan described in `SKILL.md` section 5. `keep-apart` is the only one that would
make a sensible 16:9 landing loop, because it has no chat or phone frame.

## Do not claim (carried verbatim from `SKILL.md` §4)

- ❌ **Live sync.** There is no Realtime. Collaborators see changes on reload.
  `landing.features.collab.desc` says *"zmiany synchronizują się od razu"* — the landing page
  over-claims this. Do not amplify it.
- ❌ **Plus-ones / "osoby towarzyszące".** No such field on the guest model.
  `landing.features.guests.desc` over-claims this too.
- ❌ RSVP, sending invitations, collecting guest replies.
- ❌ Offline or installable. A manifest exists; there is no service worker.
- ❌ Venue templates, or "import the venue's floor plan". That is manual founder work — a third
  landing over-claim, in `landing.steps.one.desc` (*"lub zaimportuj jej plan"*).
- ❌ A generated PDF *file*. Export opens the browser print dialog (`plan_printed` says as much).
- ❌ Free or included AI. It needs the user's own API key.
- ❌ Reminders that notify. No push, no email, no calendar. It is a dated to-do list.
- ❌ A mobile app. Budget, vendors, timeline, registry, place cards, an auto-seat button. Undo/redo.
- ❌ **Any social proof.** No counts, logos, reviews or testimonials exist, so none may be shown,
  implied, or mocked up.
- ⚠️ Free use covers planning **your own** reception. Planners and venues working commercially need
  the paid plan (`terms.technical.c6`) — so "for wedding planners" framing is off-limits.

Specific to this plan: the `mama-link` "invite" is a **collaborator** invite, not a wedding
invitation to guests (not RSVP). Mum's phone is the **website** on a phone, not an app.
